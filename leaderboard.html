HOW THE SCORE WORKS

A balanced contribution score

Each node's total score combines 50% dark-sky clarity, 30% streak consistency, and 20% blind-spot coverage. For fair comparisons, streaks cap at 30 days and coverage credits cap at 10 filled blind spots.

50%Dark-sky clarity

How clear and usable the contributed frames are.

30%Streak consistency

Regular participation, capped at a 30-day streak.

20%Blind spots filled

Coverage beyond well-observed regions, capped at 10.

Why these weights? They are tunable design choices for this concept, not a validated measure of scientific value.
